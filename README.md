# Sales Analytics KPIs - Without DAX

## Objective:
The client is experiencing limited visibility into overall sales performance across products, customers, and region. Decision making is delayed due to heavy manual reporting in Excel, inconsistent data across teams and lack of unified view of business trends. 
They require an interactive power BI dashboard that can highlight sales patterns, top contributing segments and operational gaps without using advanced calculations, ensuring faster onboarding for business users.

## KPIs:
- Sales trend Month on Month
- Sales trend by Category
- Avg. Price per Category
- Top 5 product sales
- Total Sales by Category
- Why sales decreases in Refractories
